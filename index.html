Meet Me

I'm Majemuoluwa Olowodola, an event host, compère and moderator passionate about creating experiences people remember long after the lights go out.

By profession, I'm a Product Manager working with global technology companies, where clear communication, leadership and attention to detail are part of my everyday work. On stage, those same qualities come together with warmth, confidence and a genuine love for people to create events that feel seamless, engaging and unforgettable.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of hosting corporate conferences, leadership summits, social events, awards ceremonies and community events, connecting with audiences of all sizes and ensuring every programme flows with purpose and energy.

For me, hosting goes beyond making announcements or introducing speakers. It's about setting the tone, creating meaningful connections, and making every guest feel like they're part of something special.

Because great events don't just happen. They are experienced.
